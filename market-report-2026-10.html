MARKET BALANCE

How balanced is the market?

October’s sales-to-active listings ratio was 13.4%, in the lower half of the balanced range. Detached homes, at 11.6%, were just under the 12% level usually linked to downward price pressure, while attached homes were the firmest at 16.5%.

When the ratio stays below 12%, prices tend to face downward pressure; above 20%, upward pressure. In between, the market is balanced.

OUR TAKE

What this means

October was quiet on both sides. Sales fell 10.2% from a year ago and new listings fell 11.6%, so buyers and sellers both held back. Active listings are 4.3% below last October but still 24% above the 10-year seasonal average, so buyers have more choice than in a typical October.

With a sales-to-active ratio of 13.4%, the market sits at the lower end of balanced. Detached homes are just under the 12% mark, where conditions favour buyers most; attached homes are the firmest at 16.5%.

Benchmark prices are 5.2% lower than a year ago, with detached homes down 7.0%, attached homes down 5.5% and apartments down 5.9%. On the rental side, one-bedroom asking rents across Metro Vancouver are down 6.3% from a year ago, while Vancouver two-bedrooms are up 5.2%.
