ABOUT

Hello, I’m Vivian.

Vivian has worked in property management since 2006 and holds a BSc in Statistics. When she looks at a property, she wants to know what comparable properties actually sold for, how the building has been maintained, and which details could affect the price or the terms we negotiate.

Vivian works with homeowners, buyers, investors and lenders across Metro Vancouver. Whether you’re moving from a townhouse to a house, looking at an income property or bringing a court-ordered sale to market, she’ll explain your options and stay involved as the deal moves forward. Vivian works in English and Mandarin.

SPECIALTY THREE

Court-Ordered Sales

We work with lenders and their counsel to market residential and commercial properties through court-ordered sales. Our work includes researching comparable sales, tracking showings and buyer feedback, and documenting the listing and offer history so the lender and its counsel have a clear record of what happened on the market.

If you’re considering buying a court-sale property, we can walk you through the available information and help you identify what to look into before you make an offer.

Discuss a Court-Ordered ListingAsk About Buying a Court Sale
HOW A COURT-ORDERED SALE WORKS
1
Pricing and listing. We review comparable sales and recommend a price and marketing plan.
2
Buyer inquiries and offers. We share the available property information, coordinate showings and keep a record of buyer interest and offers.
3
Court approval. We provide the marketing history and sales evidence requested by the lender and its counsel, and keep the parties updated on the sale.
4
Completion. After approval, we coordinate the remaining transaction details with the agents and lawyers involved.
