ABOUT

Hello, I’m Vivian.

I’m a REALTOR® with Royal Pacific Realty Corp. I’ve worked in property management since 2006 and hold a BSc in Statistics. When I look at a property, I want to know what comparable properties actually sold for, how the building has been maintained, and which details could affect the price or the terms we negotiate.

SPECIALTY THREE

Court-Ordered Sales

I work with lenders and their counsel to market residential and commercial properties through court-ordered sales. My work includes researching comparable sales, tracking showings and buyer feedback, and documenting the listing and offer history so the lender and its counsel have a clear record of what happened on the market.

If you’re considering buying a court-sale property, I can walk you through the available information and help you identify what to look into before you make an offer.

HOW A COURT-ORDERED SALE WORKS
1
Pricing and listing. I review comparable sales and recommend a price and marketing plan.
2
Buyer inquiries and offers. I share the available property information, coordinate showings and keep a record of buyer interest and offers.
3
Court approval. I provide the marketing history and sales evidence requested by the lender and its counsel, and keep the parties updated on the sale.
4
Completion. After approval, I coordinate the remaining transaction details with the agents and lawyers involved.
